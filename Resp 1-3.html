What Technology is Used in Sensible+?

Sensible+ uses advanced sensor technologies like electrochemical, catalytic, optical (NDIR, PID, metal oxide semiconductors), and spectroscopy to provide accurate, real-time air quality data. These technologies work in harmony to ensure that the data you receive is both reliable and actionable, helping you maintain cleaner, healthier indoor air.
